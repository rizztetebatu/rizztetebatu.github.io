    What guests love (from reviews)

    We read reviews of the best guides in Tetebatu. This is what guests write about again and again:

    Do this every tour.
    • Explain the plants. "He knows a lot about all the plants and spices." This is your strongest point.
    • Let them taste. Fresh fruit, cassava, coffee or cacao from the tree.
    • A home-made lunch. The most praised thing: lunch cooked by the guide's family at home. If you can arrange this with family or friends, it makes you stand out.
    • Be a real local. "A true local who grew up in Tetebatu." Tell your story, show your village.
    • Make them laugh and take great photos of them, on their own phone.
    • Take the safe way. The bamboo bridge instead of the slippery stairs at the Tetebatu waterfall.
    Avoid this. Guests complain about: prices that change on the spot, extra fees at the gate, a guide who barely talks, slippery paths without a warning, and being pushed to buy things. Monkeys are wild: never promise them.

      4. Photos that get bookings

      Big booking websites agree: the first photo decides. It must be horizontal and show guests having fun, not only landscape. Guests also want to see the guide's face.

      How to shoot. Hold the phone horizontal. Daylight, flash off. Wipe the lens. Tap on the face to focus. No zoom. Ask guests before you take their photo or use it.
